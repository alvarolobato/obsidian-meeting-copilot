Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 July 2026

Meeting Copilot is an open-source Obsidian plugin for macOS that helps you record, transcribe, and take notes on your meetings. It runs entirely on your own computer inside Obsidian.

There is no Meeting Copilot server. The developer does not operate any backend, does not receive your data, and cannot see your calendar, notes, recordings, or Google account.

Google user data we access

If you connect Google Calendar, the plugin requests read-only access via Google OAuth. You explicitly grant this on Google's consent screen and can revoke it at any time.

ScopeWhy we request itHow it's used
calendar.readonly Read your calendar events Show your agenda in Obsidian, create meeting notes, and prompt you around each meeting's start/end. Read-only — the plugin never creates, edits, or deletes calendar events.

Optional, advanced scopes (bring-your-own-credentials only)

If you configure the plugin with your own Google Cloud project, you may additionally grant read-only directory scopes so attendee names and group invitees resolve within your Google Workspace domain: directory.readonly (resolve a Workspace attendee's display name) and cloud-identity.groups.readonly (expand a group invitee into its members). These are not requested by the default app, only return data inside your own Workspace domain, and the plugin works without them.

How your data is stored and used

  • Everything stays on your device. Calendar data is read from Google's APIs into Obsidian on your Mac and written into your local vault as Markdown notes you control.
  • OAuth tokens are stored in Obsidian's per-vault local storage on your device only — never in synced files, never sent to the developer.
  • No developer server. The plugin talks only to Google's official endpoints (accounts.google.com, oauth2.googleapis.com, www.googleapis.com, and for the optional scopes people.googleapis.com and cloudidentity.googleapis.com).
  • We never sell, rent, or share your Google user data, and never use it for advertising, profiling, or training any AI/ML model.

Optional features that send data to services you choose

Meeting Copilot's recording, transcription, and AI-summary features are independent of Google and fully under your control:

  • Audio recordings are captured and stored locally in your vault.
  • Transcription can run entirely on-device (audio never leaves your Mac) or, if you choose, be sent to an OpenAI-compatible endpoint you configure with your own URL and API key.
  • AI enrichment, if enabled, sends the relevant note or transcript content to that same user-configured endpoint.

When you enable these, data goes to the third-party endpoint you chose, under your account and its terms — not to the plugin's author. If you use only on-device transcription and skip enrichment, nothing leaves your Mac.

Data retention and deletion

  • Notes and recordings live in your vault; delete them like any other file. Recordings can be auto-pruned by the retention setting (only after a transcript is saved).
  • Cached directory/group lookups (only if you use the optional scopes) are stored in the plugin folder and expire automatically; deleting directory-cache.json removes them immediately.
  • Removing the plugin and revoking access stops all further data access.

Revoking access

Revoke the plugin's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, or disconnect within the plugin's settings, which clears the stored tokens from your device.

Children's privacy

Meeting Copilot is a productivity tool not directed to children and does not knowingly collect data from children.
