Your meetings, captured in Obsidian.

Meeting Copilot turns your calendar into notes: it syncs Google Calendar, records both sides of the conversation, transcribes on-device or via your own endpoint, and drafts AI summaries — entirely on your Mac.

Everything a meeting note needs

From the moment an event lands on your calendar to a searchable, summarized note in your vault.

Google Calendar sync

Your agenda appears in a sidebar. Each event becomes a meeting note, and you're prompted around start and end times. Read-only — the plugin never edits your calendar.

Dual-channel recording

Captures system audio and your microphone on separate tracks, so you and the other side stay cleanly labeled in the transcript.

On-device or remote transcription

Run Whisper locally so audio never leaves your Mac, or point at any OpenAI-compatible endpoint you control. Your choice, your keys.

AI summaries

Optional enrichment drafts a summary, decisions, and action items from the transcript — which can flow into your task manager.

Meetings dashboard

A generated dashboard rolls up your meeting notes, with recording retention that tidies up audio once a transcript is saved.

Local & private

No Meeting Copilot server exists. Everything lives in your Obsidian vault on your device — nothing is sent to the developer.

How it works

Four steps from calendar invite to a finished note.

Connect your calendar

Authenticate with Google once. Meeting Copilot reads your upcoming events, read-only.

Record the meeting

Start recording from the agenda or the ribbon. Both audio channels are captured locally.

Transcribe

Whisper runs on your Mac, or your configured endpoint transcribes — with speaker labels.

Summarize & file

AI enrichment drafts a summary and action items straight into your Obsidian note.

Privacy by design

Meeting Copilot has no backend. It talks only to Google's official APIs from your own machine, reads your calendar read-only, and keeps your notes, recordings, and login tokens on your device. Read the full Privacy Policy.
