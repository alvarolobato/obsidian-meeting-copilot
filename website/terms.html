Meeting Copilot is a free, open-source Obsidian plugin for macOS. By installing or using it, you agree to these terms.

Provided as is, with no account and no server. Meeting Copilot has no backend, no login, and no connection back to the developer. Everything happens on your own Mac, inside your own vault, under your control.

No account, no server

Meeting Copilot has no backend. The developer does not operate any server, does not host your data, and has no account system, subscription, or license key. These rules can depend on where each participant is located, not just where you are, and separate rules may apply to workplace monitoring or recording public officials.
