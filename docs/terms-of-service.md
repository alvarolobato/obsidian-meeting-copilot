## The short version

Meeting Copilot is provided **as is**, for free, with no account, no server, and no
connection back to the developer. Everything the plugin does happens on your own
Mac, inside your own Obsidian vault, under your control. There is no service to
depend on, no uptime to promise, and nothing the developer can see or change on your
behalf.

## No account, no server

Meeting Copilot has no backend. The developer does not operate any server, does not
host your data, and has no account system, subscription, or license key. Nothing you
do in the plugin is visible to, or recoverable by, the developer. See the
[privacy policy](./privacy-policy.md) for exactly what data the plugin touches and
where it stays.

also depend on where each participant is located, not just where you are, and
separate rules may apply to workplace monitoring or recording public officials.
